Privacy Policy

Happy Seed · Effective November 6, 2026

Happy Seed ("the app") is published by DTS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect personal information ourselves. The mobile app shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect limited device and advertising data, and an optional online leaderboard run by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, as described below.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive information ourselves. The app has no accounts of its own, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal data. We do not run our own servers for the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score, your seed count, the time your free seed refill becomes available, your sound and language choices, and whether you have finished the first flight on your device so the game can pick up where you left off. These values never leave your device and are not sent to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games, Apple Game Center)

The mobile app has an online leaderboard. On Android it is run by Google Play Games Services, and on iOS by Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to that service on your device, the app sends your score at the end of each round, and the service shows your best score together with your player name (your Play Games or Game Center nickname) to other players. Sign-in, your player profile, and its visibility are managed by Google or Apple, not by us, and are governed by their privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in; your scores are then not shared.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The mobile app lets you watch an optional rewarded video ad to earn extra seeds. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalized. We do not receive this information from Google in a form that identifies you.

Learn more about how Google handles this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • iOS: the app asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Europe, UK, and other regions where required: the app shows a consent message the first time it starts, and you can change your choice later from the "Ad privacy choices" button on the in-app Privacy screen.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Watching ads is optional. You can instead wait for free seeds to refill.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files.

Network use

The game itself works offline. An internet connection is only used to send scores to and load the leaderboard, and by the advertising service when you choose to watch a rewarded video ad.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data processed by Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, or Apple Game Center is governed by Google's or Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
