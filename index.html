How to play

Ride the summer wind on a dandelion seed. Each flight is one day, from sunrise to sunset.

  • Press and hold anywhere: a gust lifts the seed. Let go and it drifts down.
  • Touch the people and animals in the meadow to cheer them up.
  • Touch one after another without a miss to build a combo. Through the middle scores more.
  • Touch the grass and the flight is over.
  • Every flight uses one seed. Out of seeds? Wait for free ones or watch a short video.
